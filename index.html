Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere. The dish is found by looking for the largest patch of saturated colour (media) against the paler background; very pale/clear media or a very colourful background surface can throw this off, in which case nudge the sensitivity slider. Overlapping colonies that visually merge into one blob are counted as one. Glare on the media is filtered by shape (a real colony is a filled dot, not a hollow ring) and, optionally, by requiring actual colour rather than near-white brightness.
